# CEO Personal Operating System

A private, single-user productivity system designed for clarity, not complexity.

---

## What This Is

This is your personal operating system for the year ahead. It's a collection of plain-text files that help you:

- Reflect on what happened
- Define what matters
- Check in daily and weekly
- Course-correct quarterly
- Plan and review annually
- Store insights that compound over time

This is not software. It's a system. You can run it with nothing but a text editor, or pair it with the companion iOS app for a more guided experience.

---

## Philosophy

**Less is more.** This system deliberately avoids dashboards, metrics, and productivity theater. The goal is clarity, not activity.

**You are the system.** These files are mirrors, not managers. They help you see clearly—they don't tell you what to do.

**Compounding insight.** Every reflection, every review, every pattern noticed—they accumulate in `memory.md` and inform future decisions.

**Executive-level thinking.** The prompts here are designed for someone making high-stakes decisions daily. They're calm, direct, and assume you're competent.

## How to Use This System

### Initial Setup (15 minutes)

1. **Read `principles.md`** — Understand the philosophy. Edit if it doesn't resonate.

2. **Complete `north_star.md`** — Answer the five questions. This anchors everything.

3. **Review the frameworks in `/frameworks/`** — Familiarize yourself with the thinking models. You don't need to complete them today.

4. **Optional: Upload past documents** — Drop any past annual reviews, performance reviews, or personal reflections into `/uploads/`. The system will help you extract patterns from them.

5. **Set your goals** — Complete at least `goals/1_year.md` to start. The 3-year and 10-year versions can wait until your first quarterly review.

---

### Daily Use (5 minutes)

Every morning or evening, complete a daily check-in:

**Location:** `/reviews/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`

The iOS app provides a guided 5-minute flow. Or copy the template from `/reviews/daily/_template.md` and fill it in manually.

A daily check-in includes:
- Energy level (1-10)
- One meaningful win
- One friction point
- One thing to let go of
- One priority for tomorrow

That's it. No journaling pressure. No elaborate tracking.

---

### Weekly Use (30 minutes)

Every weekend (Saturday or Sunday), complete a weekly review:

**Location:** `/reviews/weekly/YYYY-WXX.md`

This covers:
- What moved the needle this week?
- What was noise?
- Where did time leak?
- One strategic insight
- One adjustment for next week

---

### Quarterly Use (2-3 hours)

At the end of each quarter (March, June, September, December), complete a quarterly review:

**Location:** `/reviews/quarterly/YYYY-QX.md`

This includes:
- Goal progress check
- Energy vs. output analysis
- Misalignment detection
- Course correction decisions

This is also a good time to revisit your Life Map (`/frameworks/life_map.md`) and check if your energy is flowing to the right areas.

---

### Annual Use (Half day to full day)

At year-end (or your chosen annual milestone), complete the full annual cycle:

1. **Past Year Reflection Interview** — `/interviews/past_year_reflection.md`
2. **Annual Review Framework** — `/frameworks/annual_review.md`
3. **Update Life Map** — `/frameworks/life_map.md`
4. **Revisit Ideal Lifestyle Costing** — `/frameworks/ideal_life_costing.md`
5. **Refresh Vivid Vision** — `/frameworks/vivid_vision.md`
6. **Set new goals** — Update all files in `/goals/`
7. **Write annual summary** — `/reviews/annual/YYYY.md`
8. **Update memory** — Add key insights to `memory.md`

## The iOS App

The companion iOS app provides:

- **Guided daily check-ins** with a calm, 5-minute flow
- **Weekly review interface** with prompts and previous context
- **Interview mode** for deeper reflection sessions
- **Pattern insights** from your accumulated data

## Assumptions Built Into This System

This system was designed with certain assumptions. If they don't fit, adapt accordingly:

1. **You are a CEO, founder, or senior operator.** The questions and frameworks are calibrated for someone making organization-level decisions.

2. **You have limited time for personal reflection.** Everything is designed to be efficient. Daily check-ins are 5 minutes. Weekly reviews are 30 minutes.

3. **You value clarity over complexity.** There are no metrics, no dashboards, no gamification. Just thinking prompts and plain text.

4. **You're willing to be honest with yourself.** These frameworks only work if you engage with them truthfully.

5. **Your life has multiple important domains.** Career is important, but so are relationships, health, meaning, and fun.

6. **Past patterns predict future patterns.** That's why we upload past reviews and look for themes.

7. **You're playing a long game.** 10-year thinking is included because short-term optimization can lead to long-term regret.

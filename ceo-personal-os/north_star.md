## Using Your North Star

These answers should inform:
- **Goal setting** — Are your goals aligned with your North Star?

- **Course correction** — When life drifts, return here to recalibrate
- **Saying no** — Opportunities that don't align can be declined confidently

Review quarterly. Update annually. Your North Star can evolve as you do.

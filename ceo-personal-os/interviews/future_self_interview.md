# Future Self Interview

*A conversation with who you're becoming*

---

## What This Is

This is an imaginative exercise where you interview your future self—the version of you that exists 10 years from now, having navigated the next decade successfully.

This isn't fantasy. It's a way to access wisdom you already have but haven't articulated yet.

**When to use:** Annual planning, major decisions, or when feeling stuck

**Time:** 45-60 minutes

**Approach:** Take it seriously. Your future self knows things your current self has forgotten or hasn't yet realized.

---

## Setting the Scene

Close your eyes for a moment.

It's 10 years from today. You've lived the next decade. Some things went as planned. Many didn't. But here you are—older, wiser, still standing.

Picture yourself at 10-years-older. Where are you? What do you look like? What's the expression on your face?

When you're ready, begin the conversation.

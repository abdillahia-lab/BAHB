# Past Year Reflection Interview

*A guided conversation with yourself about the year that was*

---

## How to Use This

This is designed to feel like a conversation with a thoughtful friend or coach—someone who asks good questions and holds space for honest answers.

**Setting:** Find a quiet place. No interruptions. Have your calendar and any notes from the year nearby.

**Time:** 60-90 minutes

**Approach:** Answer out loud or in writing. Don't edit yourself. Let it flow.

---

## Opening

Take three deep breaths. Let the year come back to you—not the highlight reel, but the actual texture of the days, the decisions, the feelings.

When you're ready, begin.

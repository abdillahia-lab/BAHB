# Vivid Vision

*Adapted from Tony Robbins-style future state design*

---

## What This Is

A Vivid Vision is a detailed, sensory description of your life at a specific point in the future—written as if it has already happened.

This isn't goal-setting. It's reality creation. You're writing a memory of a future that hasn't occurred yet, in enough detail that your brain treats it as real.

**Why It Works:** The brain doesn't fully distinguish between vividly imagined experiences and real ones. A clear, detailed vision acts as a GPS coordinate. Decisions become easier when you know where you're going.

**Time Required:** 1-2 hours for first draft. Refine annually.

**When to Use:** During annual planning, or anytime you need to reconnect with your direction.

---

## Instructions

1. Choose a time horizon (3 years is optimal—far enough to dream, close enough to feel real)
2. Write in present tense, as if it's already true ("I am..." not "I will...")
3. Be specific and sensory—describe what you see, feel, hear
4. Don't censor yourself—this is private
5. Cover all life domains, not just work

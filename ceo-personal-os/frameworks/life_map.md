# Life Map

*Adapted from Alex Lieberman's Life Map framework*

---

## What This Is

Your life operates across six interconnected domains. This map helps you see them clearly—not to obsess over "balance," but to ensure you're not accidentally neglecting something that matters.

**The six domains:**
1. **Career** — Work, professional growth, impact
2. **Relationships** — Partner, family, friends, community
3. **Health** — Physical, mental, energy
4. **Meaning** — Purpose, contribution, spirituality
5. **Finances** — Money, security, freedom
6. **Fun** — Joy, adventure, play

**The insight:** Life satisfaction comes from having enough in each domain, not from maximizing any single one.

**Time Required:** 30-60 minutes

**When to Use:** Quarterly reviews, annual planning, or when something feels off

# Ideal Lifestyle Costing

*Adapted from Tim Ferriss's lifestyle design methodology*

---

## What This Is

Most people optimize for money without knowing what they need money for. This exercise reverses the equation:

1. Design your ideal lifestyle in specific detail
2. Calculate what it actually costs
3. Discover the gap between fear and reality

**The insight:** Most dream lifestyles cost less than people assume. And earning is often not the bottleneck—permission is.

**Time Required:** 1-2 hours for full exercise

**When to Use:** Annual planning, major life transitions, or when feeling trapped by financial constraints

---

## The Key Question

**What would you do if you couldn't fail and money wasn't an obstacle?**

Don't answer with what you *should* want. Answer with what you actually want.

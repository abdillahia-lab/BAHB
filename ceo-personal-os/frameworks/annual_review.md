# Annual Review Framework

*Adapted from Dr. Anthony Gustin's Annual Review methodology*

---

## What This Is

This is a structured framework for reviewing the past year with clarity and depth. It's designed to help you:

- See the year honestly, not through a highlight reel
- Extract lessons that can inform future decisions
- Identify patterns you might otherwise miss
- Close out the year consciously before starting a new one

**Time Required:** 2-3 hours minimum. Best done in a quiet space without interruption.

**When to Use:** December or your chosen year-end milestone.

---

## Before You Begin

1. Have your calendar from the past year accessible
2. Gather any journals, notes, or records from the year
3. Review past quarterly reviews if available
4. Have your 1-year goals from last year visible

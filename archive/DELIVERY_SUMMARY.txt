THE INSIGHT
================================================================================

PROBLEM:
  Jinki Intelligence has sophisticated capabilities (voice AI, chatbot, 
  personalization, lead qualification) that live in parallel silos. 
  Enterprise buyers experience them as separate tools, not one platform.
  
  - Voice context is lost when switching to chat
  - Lead data isn't reused when filling forms
  - Responses feel generic, not personalized
  - No seamless experience across modalities
  
  Result: Enterprise buyer friction, reduced conversion, extended sales cycles

SOLUTION:
  Conversational Continuity Layer—orchestrate existing features into one 
  seamless experience:
  
  ✓ Voice input flows directly to chat (no context loss)
  ✓ Lead qualification data auto-populates forms (no re-entry)
  ✓ Personalization adapts responses to role/industry (intelligence signal)
  ✓ Seamless mode switching (voice ↔ text)
  
  Result: Professional platform feel, improved conversion, faster qualification

WHY IT WINS:

  ✓ Feasible execution (4.3 hours, proven approach)

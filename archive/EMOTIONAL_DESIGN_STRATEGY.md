## 4. COPY TONE RECOMMENDATIONS
### Language Strategy to Build Enterprise Confidence

### **A. TONE PILLARS**

```
PRIMARY TONE: CONFIDENT + REASSURING
├─ Confidence: Assert capabilities without hyperbole
├─ Reassurance: Show we understand risk and have solved it
├─ Authority: Speak from expertise, not features
└─ Clarity: Enterprise buyers have zero time for jargon

SECONDARY TONE: INNOVATION + PROVEN
├─ Innovation: We're not just doing drones, we're reinventing inspection
├─ Proven: But we're battle-tested, not experimental
└─ Human: Security expert backing, not just AI

├─ "Game-changing" (every startup says this)
├─ "Cutting-edge" (vague)
├─ "Revolutionary" (red flag to enterprises)
├─ Excessive exclamation marks (unprofessional)
```

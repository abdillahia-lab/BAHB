TIMING HIERARCHY (Durations):
  • INSTANT (100ms) - Fastest feedback
  • FAST (150ms) - Quick reactions
  • QUICK (200ms) - Snappy interactions
  • NORMAL (300ms) - Standard movements
  • MEDIUM (400ms) - Deliberate movements
  • SLOW (500ms) - Slow reveals
  • LEISURELY (700ms) - Gentle animations
  • EPIC (1000ms) - Grand entrances

EASING FUNCTIONS (Personalities):
  • MATERIAL_STANDARD - Professional, balanced
  • MATERIAL_DECELERATE - Smooth exits
  • MATERIAL_ACCELERATE - Sharp entries
  • SNAP - Quick snap to place
  • ELASTIC - Playful, bouncy
  • BOUNCE_OUT - Lands with bounce
  • ANTICIPATION - Wind-up before action
  • OVERSHOOT - Overshoots then settles
  • LINEAR, EASE_IN, EASE_OUT, EASE_IN_OUT

SPRING PRESETS (Personalities):
  • snappy (stiffness: 500, damping: 20) - Quick, responsive
  • bouncy (stiffness: 300, damping: 10) - Playful, elastic
  • smooth (stiffness: 300, damping: 25) - Elegant, controlled
  • gentle (stiffness: 200, damping: 30) - Subtle, understated
  • molasses (stiffness: 100, damping: 40) - Slow, heavy

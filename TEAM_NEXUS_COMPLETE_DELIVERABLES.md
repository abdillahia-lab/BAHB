## THE FOUR PILLARS OF THE PROPOSAL

### 1. INSIGHT (Problem)
Enterprise buyers lose context when switching between voice and chat. They re-enter data even though the system already extracted it. Responses feel generic, not personalized. This friction depresses conversion and extends sales cycles.

### 2. SOLUTION (What We Build)
A Conversational Continuity Layer that orchestrates existing capabilities:
- Voice input flows to chat (no context loss)
- Lead qualification data auto-populates forms (no re-entry)
- Personalization adapts responses (industry/role-aware)

### 3. EXECUTION (How We Build It)
5.8 hours of integration work (pure orchestration):
- New orchestrator component
- Voice input wiring
- Personalization injection
- Form auto-fill bridge

### 4. IMPACT (Why It Wins)
- Measurable business results (conversion lift, lead quality)
- Psychological positioning ("understands enterprise workflows")
- Competitive moat (architecture-level, not feature-level)
- Elegant simplicity (fewer bugs, faster to market)

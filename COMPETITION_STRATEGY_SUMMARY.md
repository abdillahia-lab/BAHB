The judges will score on:

1. **Information Clarity** (30 points)
   - How quickly can user understand value?

   - How many decision points force user to think?
   - Are CTAs clear and singular or confusing and multiple?

   - Is all information thrown at user at once?

   - Are details available on-demand?

5. **Measurable Impact** (10 points)
   - Can you prove your design reduces cognitive load?
   - Do you have before/after metrics?
   - Is user effort quantified?

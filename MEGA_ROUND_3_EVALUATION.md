**Why Bottom 6 Failed:**

- **QUANTUMGLASS**: Trend-dependent glassmorphism becoming saturated, accessibility problems
- **HYPERSONIC**: Speed metaphor doesn't connect to Jinki's vision/AI core mission
- **SIGNALPATH**: Too engineering-niche, lacks emotional breadth for $50M deals

- **COMMANDLINE**: Mobile disaster, alienates 80% of decision-makers, accessibility failure
- **HOLOMAP**: Surveying/topography has zero connection to AI/vision/cyber-eye brand

**Why Top 6 Advanced:**

All six advancing concepts share:
1. Strong connection to Jinki's cyber-eye/vision/AI brand identity
2. Balance of technical credibility + emotional resonance
3. Viable mobile experience (even if challenging)
4. Clear path to $50M deal credibility
5. Distinctive competitive moat
6. 8+ scores on either timelessness OR memorability

The top 6 represent different strategic approaches:
- **NEURALNET/BIOMIMETIC**: AI-native, algorithmic intelligence
- **AEROSPACE/VELOCITYHUD**: Precision targeting, advanced systems
- **CRYSTALLATTICE**: Eternal geometric clarity
- **TRANSCENDENT**: Confident minimalist authority
